---

## New Standard

All future work logs follow a concise professional pattern:

**Required Sections** (8 maximum):
1. Header (Title, Date, Task ID, Phase, Duration, Status)
2. Executive Summary (5-8 sentences)
3. Problem Statement (brief and factual)
4. Decisions Made (bullet points)
5. Actions Taken (concise list)
6. Artifacts Affected (files added/modified/deleted)
7. Validation (how correctness verified)
8. Next Steps (if applicable)

**Constraint**: Must fit on small number of screens. Focus on decisions and outcomes, not narrative or step-by-step transcripts.
