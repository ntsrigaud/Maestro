## Discussions

- Presentation of the findings from the research paper: [GESTOP: Customizable Gesture Control of Computer Systems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.13197v1)
  - [Research Notes on GESTOP](../Research-Notes/GESTOP.md)
  - GESTOP control desktop through hand gestures
    - Same as what we are doing.
    - Fully functional replacement for the mouse.
    - User can add gestures and actions.
  - GESTOP uses Mediapipe 21 landmarks for HGR
  - GESTOP needs to collect data from users, about ~15 minutes before completely retraining model to detect new gesture.
    - No zero-shot learning capability.
    - Training uses 50 epochs.
    - Lower performance on dynamic gestures (85% average accuracy)
    - Need to compute start/end gesture precisely for dynamic gestures
      - Use of **_signal key `CTRL`_** to mark them.
  - Their future work involves improving detection accuracy.
